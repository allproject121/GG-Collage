Certificate & Add-on Courses

Enhance your skills and employability with our value-added programmes

Certificate in Web Development

Duration: 3 Months | 60 Hours

HTML5, CSS3, JavaScript, Bootstrap and responsive web design. Build and deploy your own website as a final project.

Eligibility: Any UG/PG student

Certificate in Financial Accounting

Duration: 3 Months | 60 Hours

Hands-on training in Tally ERP, GST filing, payroll accounting and financial statement preparation.

Eligibility: B.Com / BMS / M.Com students

Certificate in Data Analytics

Duration: 6 Months | 120 Hours

Introduction to Python, SQL, Excel analytics, data visualization and business intelligence tools.

Eligibility: Any UG/PG student

Certificate in Spoken English & Communication

Duration: 2 Months | 40 Hours

Improve English speaking, writing, presentation skills and interview techniques through interactive sessions.

Eligibility: All students

Certificate in Digital Photography

Duration: 2 Months | 40 Hours

Learn composition, lighting, editing using Adobe Lightroom and Photoshop, and build a photography portfolio.

Eligibility: All students

Certificate in Entrepreneurship

Duration: 3 Months | 60 Hours

Business planning, market research, financial modeling, legal aspects and pitch deck preparation for aspiring entrepreneurs.

Eligibility: Any UG/PG student

Certificate in AI & Machine Learning

Duration: 6 Months | 120 Hours

Fundamentals of AI, machine learning algorithms, neural networks, and hands-on projects using Python libraries.

Eligibility: B.Sc. IT / Data Science / BCA students

Certificate in Environmental Studies

Duration: 2 Months | 30 Hours

Understanding ecosystems, climate change, sustainability practices and environmental impact assessment.

Eligibility: All students

Certificate in Yoga & Wellness

Duration: 2 Months | 30 Hours

Physical postures, breathing techniques, meditation and stress management for overall well-being.

Eligibility: All students
