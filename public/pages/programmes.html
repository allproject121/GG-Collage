Programme Outcomes (POs)

Graduate attributes that all students acquire upon completion of their chosen programme

PO1

Critical Thinking

Ability to analyse, evaluate, and synthesise information from diverse sources to form reasoned judgments.

PO2

Communication Skills

Demonstrate effective oral, written, and digital communication in academic and professional contexts.

PO3

Problem Solving

Apply disciplinary knowledge and analytical skills to identify, formulate, and solve real-world problems.

PO4

Digital & IT Literacy

Proficient use of modern ICT tools, software applications, and digital platforms relevant to the field.

PO5

Ethical & Social Responsibility

Understand and uphold ethical values, professional integrity, and commitment to social well-being.

PO6

Teamwork & Leadership

Work effectively in diverse teams, demonstrate leadership qualities, and contribute to collaborative goals.

PO7

Life-long Learning

Recognise the need for continuous learning and adapt to evolving personal and professional demands.

PO8

Research & Innovation

Develop a research-oriented mindset, apply inquiry-based methods, and contribute to knowledge creation.
