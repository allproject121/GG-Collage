About NIRF

The National Institutional Ranking Framework (NIRF) uses a set of parameters for ranking institutions across India. These include Teaching, Learning & Resources, Research & Professional Practice, Graduation Outcomes, Outreach & Inclusivity, and Perception.

St. Gonsalo Garcia College is committed to transparency and continuous improvement, and the NIRF data provides valuable insights into our institutional performance across these key dimensions.
