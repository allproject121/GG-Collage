Women Development Cell

Empowering women through education, awareness, and skill development

About the Cell

The Women Development Cell (WDC) at St. Gonsalo Garcia College is committed to the empowerment and holistic development of women students and staff. The cell organizes various programs, workshops, and awareness campaigns focused on women's rights, health, safety, and career development.

The cell works towards creating a safe and supportive environment for women on campus and addresses issues related to gender discrimination, harassment, and inequality.

Objectives

Educational Empowerment

To promote educational opportunities and academic excellence among women students.

Safety & Security

To ensure a safe and secure campus environment for women through awareness and support systems.

Skill Development

To conduct skill development workshops and training programs for career advancement.

Gender Equality

To promote gender equality and address issues of discrimination and harassment.

Health & Wellness

To organize health awareness camps and wellness programs for women.

Legal Awareness

To create awareness about legal rights and provisions available for women.
