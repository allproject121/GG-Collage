National Education Policy (NEP) 2020

Implementation of NEP 2020 at GG College

St. Gonsalo Garcia College is committed to the effective implementation of the National Education Policy (NEP) 2020. The college has adopted the NEP curriculum framework as prescribed by the University of Mumbai, focusing on a multidisciplinary approach, flexibility in course choices, and skill-based learning.

Key features of NEP implementation at GG College include:

  • Multiple Entry and Exit options for undergraduate programmes
  • Choice-Based Credit System (CBCS) with wide range of electives
  • Focus on skill-based courses and vocational training
  • Indian Knowledge System (IKS) integration in curriculum
  • Research orientation through research methodology courses
  • Continuous internal assessment combined with semester-end examinations
