Revaluation Process

Procedure for applying for revaluation or photocopy of answer scripts

Revaluation / Photocopy Application Procedure

  1. Results are published on the University of Mumbai website and college notice board.
  2. Students who wish to apply for revaluation or obtain a photocopy of their answer script must fill the prescribed application form available at the college exam office.
  3. The application form must be submitted along with the prescribed fee as per University of Mumbai norms.
  4. Applications must be submitted within 10 working days from the date of result declaration.
  5. The college examination office will forward the applications to the University of Mumbai for processing.
  6. Revaluation results are typically declared within 30–45 days from the date of application.
  7. If the revaluation results in a change of marks, the updated marks will be reflected in the student's academic record.

Examination Rules

Guidelines and regulations to be followed during examinations

General Examination Rules

  • Students must carry their college ID card and hall ticket to every examination.
  • Entry to the examination hall is permitted only up to 15 minutes after the start of the exam.
  • No electronic gadgets (mobile phones, smartwatches, etc.) are allowed inside the examination hall.
  • Students must occupy the seats allotted to them as per the seating arrangement displayed at the exam hall.
  • Use of unfair means or malpractice will result in strict disciplinary action as per University of Mumbai ordinances.
  • Students must write their seat number clearly on the answer booklet and all supplements.
  • No additional sheets or rough paper will be provided beyond the answer booklet and authorized supplements.
  • Students must not leave the examination hall during the first hour and the last 15 minutes of the exam.
  • Answer booklets must be handed over to the invigilator before leaving the hall.
  • Students should follow the dress code prescribed by the college during examinations.
