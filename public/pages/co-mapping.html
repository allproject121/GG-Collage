CO-PO Mapping Process

Understanding how Course Outcomes align with Programme Outcomes

What is CO-PO Mapping?

CO-PO Mapping establishes the relationship between Course Outcomes (COs) and Programme Outcomes (POs). Each CO is mapped to one or more POs with correlation levels: Low (1), Medium (2) or High (3). This ensures every course contributes meaningfully to programme goals.

Mapping Scale

Correlation LevelValueDescription
High (Strong)3Direct and strong relationship. Significant contribution.
Medium (Moderate)2Moderate relationship. Noticeable contribution.
Low (Slight)1Slight or indirect relationship. Minor contribution.
No Correlation-No correlation between CO and PO.

Attainment Calculation Process

  1. Set Targets: Define threshold for each CO attainment.
  2. Collect Data: Gather internal and external assessment scores mapped to each CO.
  3. Calculate CO Attainment: Percentage of students achieving target level for each CO.
  4. Calculate PO Attainment: Weighted average of CO attainments mapped to each PO.
  5. Compare and Improve: Identify gaps and take corrective actions in curriculum, pedagogy or assessment.

Formula for PO Attainment

PO Attainment = Σ(CO Attainment × Mapping Strength) / Σ(Mapping Strength)

Benefits of CO-PO Mapping

Curriculum Alignment

Ensures every course contributes meaningfully to programme goals.

Quality Improvement

Identifies gaps and enables continuous improvement of teaching and assessment.

Accreditation Readiness

Aligns with NAAC and NBA outcome-based education requirements for accreditation.
