Admission Process

Your journey to becoming a part of St. Gonsalo Garcia College

Step 1: Application

Fill out the online application form available on the admission portal. Ensure all details are accurate and complete.

Step 2: Document Upload

Upload scanned copies of all required documents including mark sheets, ID proof, and passport-size photographs.

Step 3: Verification

Your application and documents will be verified by the college administration. You may be called for document verification.

Step 4: Merit List

Merit lists are published based on academic performance and reservation criteria as per government norms.

Step 5: Fee Payment

Confirm your admission by paying the prescribed fees online within the given deadline.

Step 6: Enrollment

Complete the enrollment formalities and receive your college ID card and access to student resources.

Eligibility Criteria

Minimum requirements for admission to various programmes

Programme Eligibility Duration
B.A. (Bachelor of Arts) HSC (10+2) or equivalent from a recognised board with minimum 45% marks 3 Years
B.Com (Bachelor of Commerce) HSC (10+2) or equivalent with Commerce stream, minimum 45% marks 3 Years
B.Com (Accounting & Finance) HSC (10+2) or equivalent with Commerce, minimum 45% marks 3 Years
B.Com (Banking & Insurance) HSC (10+2) or equivalent with minimum 45% marks 3 Years
BMS (Bachelor of Management Studies) HSC (10+2) or equivalent with minimum 45% marks 3 Years
B.Sc. IT (Information Technology) HSC (10+2) with Science stream, minimum 45% marks 3 Years
B.Sc. Data Science HSC (10+2) with Science/Commerce with Mathematics, minimum 45% marks 3 Years
M.A. (Master of Arts) Graduation in relevant discipline with minimum 50% marks 2 Years
M.Com (Master of Commerce) B.Com or equivalent with minimum 50% marks 2 Years
M.Sc. IT (Information Technology) B.Sc. IT or BCA or equivalent with minimum 50% marks 2 Years
Ph.D. Rural Development Master's degree with minimum 55% marks, NET/SET/M.Phil qualified As per UGC norms

Documents Required

List of documents to be submitted at the time of admission

Mandatory Documents

  • SSC (10th) Mark Sheet and Passing Certificate
  • HSC (12th) Mark Sheet and Passing Certificate
  • Graduation Mark Sheets (for PG / Ph.D. applicants)
  • School/College Leaving Certificate (Transfer Certificate)
  • Migration Certificate (for students from other boards/universities)
  • Aadhaar Card / Government ID Proof
  • Passport-size Photographs (4 copies)
  • Caste/Category Certificate (if applicable)
  • Income Certificate (if applicable)
  • Gap Affidavit (if there is a gap in education)

Additional Documents

  • Domicile Certificate (if applicable)
  • Sports / NCC / NSS Certificate (if seeking reservation)
  • Disability Certificate (if applicable)
  • EWS Certificate (Economically Weaker Section)
  • Anti-Ragging Affidavit (to be submitted online)
  • ABC ID (Academic Bank of Credits registration)
  • Character Certificate from last institution attended
  • Bonafide Certificate (for in-service candidates)
