Rules and Code of Conduct

All students are expected to adhere to the following rules and code of conduct to maintain discipline and a conducive learning environment.

  • Students must wear their college ID cards at all times while on campus.
  • Prescribed dress code must be followed strictly.
  • Attendance of minimum 75% is mandatory in each subject to appear for examinations.
  • Use of mobile phones is prohibited in classrooms and examination halls.
  • Ragging in any form is strictly prohibited and punishable under law.
  • Students must maintain decorum and respect towards faculty, staff, and fellow students.
  • Damage to college property will result in disciplinary action and fine.
  • Participation in co-curricular and extra-curricular activities is encouraged.
  • Any form of discrimination based on caste, creed, gender, or religion is not tolerated.
  • Students must abide by the college's anti-ragging and anti-sexual harassment policies.

Guidelines for Viewing Results

  1. Visit the University of Mumbai website.
  2. Navigate to the "Results" section from the homepage.
  3. Select your faculty (Arts / Commerce / Science) and programme.
  4. Enter your seat number / PRN and other required details.
  5. Click on "View Result" to display your marksheet.
  6. Download and save a copy for future reference.

Note: Results published on the college website are for reference only. Official marksheets will be issued by the University of Mumbai.

ICT Enabled Teaching

The college leverages Information and Communication Technology (ICT) to enhance the teaching-learning experience.

  • Smart classrooms with LCD projectors and audio-visual aids.
  • Computer laboratories with high-speed internet and updated software.
  • Learning Management System (LMS) for sharing notes, assignments, and announcements.
  • Digital library access through the Knowledge Resource Centre (KRC).
  • Recorded lectures and online resources available for revision.
  • Use of Google Classroom, Zoom, and other platforms for blended learning.

Admitted Students

Information and resources for newly admitted students to help you get started at GG College.

Orientation

New students are invited to the orientation programme at the beginning of the academic session. Details will be communicated via email and notice board.

Student ID Card

College ID cards will be issued after completion of admission formalities. Carry your ID card at all times on campus.

Registered students can access the college library and the digital Knowledge Resource Centre (KRC).

College Email

Students are provided with a college email ID for official communication. Activate your email through the IT department.

Facilities for Physically Challenged Students

The college is committed to providing an inclusive and accessible environment for students with disabilities.

  • Ramps and wheelchair-friendly pathways at all building entrances.
  • Accessible washrooms on the ground floor.
  • Preferential seating in classrooms and examination halls.
  • Assistance during exams including scribe facility as per university norms.
  • Library services including book delivery and assistive technology.
  • Separate counselling and mentoring support for differently-abled students.
  • Elevator access to upper floors.

Mentoring

The college has a structured mentoring system where each student is assigned a faculty mentor for academic and personal guidance.

  • Each mentor handles a group of 15–20 students.
  • Regular mentoring sessions are conducted to review academic progress.
  • Mentors provide guidance on career choices, study techniques, and personal development.
  • Students can approach their mentor for any academic or personal concerns.
  • Mentoring records are maintained and reviewed by the IQAC.

ABC ID (Academic Bank of Credits)

As per NEP 2020, all students must create an Academic Bank of Credits (ABC) ID. This ID enables seamless credit transfer across institutions.

  1. Visit the ABC Portal.
  2. Register using your Aadhaar number and academic details.
  3. Generate your unique ABC ID.
  4. Submit your ABC ID to the college administration for verification and linking.
