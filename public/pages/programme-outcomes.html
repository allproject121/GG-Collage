Programme Outcomes (POs)

Programme Outcomes describe the knowledge, skills and attitudes acquired upon completion of a programme.

What are Programme Outcomes?

Programme Outcomes (POs) are statements describing what students know and can do at the end of a programme. They are aligned with the institutions vision and graduate attributes, forming the basis for curriculum design and assessment strategies.

Generic Programme Outcomes (Arts, Commerce & Science)

PO1: Disciplinary Knowledge

Demonstrate comprehensive knowledge of core concepts and theories in the chosen discipline.

PO2: Critical Thinking

Analyze and synthesize information to form reasoned judgments and solve complex problems.

PO3: Communication Skills

Communicate effectively in verbal and written forms in academic and professional contexts.

PO4: Analytical and Research Skills

Apply research methodologies to investigate problems and draw evidence-based conclusions.

PO5: Digital and ICT Skills

Use modern digital tools effectively for learning, research and professional activities.

PO6: Ethical and Social Responsibility

Demonstrate ethical awareness and commitment to sustainable and inclusive practices.

PO7: Individual and Team Work

Work effectively independently and as a member of diverse teams.

PO8: Lifelong Learning

Recognize the need for continuous self-directed professional development.

Programme-Specific Outcomes

B.A. (Bachelor of Arts)

  • Deep understanding of social, political and cultural dimensions of society.
  • Analytical and interpretive skills through literature, history and social sciences.
  • Apply theoretical frameworks to real-world community issues.
  • Enhance language proficiency and communication skills.

B.Com (Bachelor of Commerce)

  • Comprehensive knowledge of accounting, finance, taxation and business law.
  • Skills in financial analysis, budgeting and risk assessment.
  • Understanding of business environment and corporate governance.
  • Preparation for careers in accounting, banking and financial services.

B.Sc. IT (Information Technology)

  • Proficiency in programming, databases, networking and web technologies.
  • Apply software engineering principles to develop solutions.
  • Problem-solving using algorithmic thinking and computation.
  • Preparation for careers in software development and IT consulting.

B.Sc. Data Science

  • Statistical methods, machine learning algorithms and big data technologies.
  • Collect, analyze and visualize data for actionable insights.
  • Build predictive models for business and scientific problems.
  • Preparation for careers as data analysts and scientists.

M.Com and Ph.D. Rural Development

  • Advanced financial management, strategic management and research.
  • Contribute original research to rural development and allied fields.
  • Develop policy recommendations for rural transformation.
