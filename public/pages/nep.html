NEP 2020 at St. Gonsalo Garcia College

Embracing the transformative National Education Policy 2020

Overview

The National Education Policy (NEP) 2020 is a landmark reform that aims to transform India education system into a more holistic, flexible and multidisciplinary framework. St. Gonsalo Garcia College is committed to implementing NEP 2020 in both spirit and practice.

The policy emphasizes critical thinking, creativity, communication skills and ethical values alongside academic excellence, introducing a flexible credit-based system with multiple entry and exit options.

Key Features of NEP 2020

Holistic & Multidisciplinary Education

Students choose subjects across disciplines, breaking traditional boundaries of arts, commerce and science.

Multiple Entry & Exit Options

Certification at 1 year (Certificate), 2 years (Diploma), 3 years (Degree) or 4 years (Honours).

Academic Bank of Credits

Digital credit account for storing and transferring credits across institutions.

Skill-Based Learning

Vocational education through internships, apprenticeships and skill courses.

Indian Language & Culture

Promotes Indian languages and integrates traditional knowledge systems into the curriculum.

Innovative Pedagogy

Shift from rote to experiential learning with project-based and research-oriented activities.

Credit Distribution

  • Major (Core): 50% of credits
  • Minor: 20% of credits
  • Multidisciplinary: 10%
  • Ability Enhancement: Language courses
  • Skill Enhancement: Vocational courses
  • Value Added: Indian knowledge systems
  • Internship: Practical exposure

Major & Minor Structure

Major (Core)

The primary specialization pursued in-depth. 88 credits over 4 years. Examples: Economics, Accountancy, IT.

Minor (Interdisciplinary)

A secondary area complementing the major. 32 credits over 4 years. Examples: Data Analysis, Psychology.

Implementation at Our College

Key steps: curriculum redesign per CBCS, ABC registration, faculty development, skill courses, and outcome-based assessment.
