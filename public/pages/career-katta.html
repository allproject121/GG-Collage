Career Katta

Career guidance and counseling for students

About Career Katta

Career Katta is a dedicated career guidance and counseling initiative at St. Gonsalo Garcia College. It aims to help students make informed career choices, develop essential skills, and prepare for the competitive job market.

The cell organizes workshops, seminars, and counseling sessions to guide students towards suitable career paths based on their interests and aptitudes.

Services Offered

Career Counseling

One-on-one counseling sessions to help students identify their career goals and aspirations.

Resume Building

Workshops on resume writing, cover letter preparation, and LinkedIn profile optimization.

Interview Preparation

Mock interviews and training sessions to prepare students for job interviews.

Skill Development

Training programs on communication skills, soft skills, and professional etiquette.

Job Placement

Assistance in finding internship and job opportunities through industry connections.

Higher Education

Guidance on higher education options, entrance exams, and scholarship opportunities.

Workshops & Events

Quarterly

Career Guidance Workshop

Workshops on various career options and industry trends.

Monthly

Industry Expert Talks

Guest lectures by industry professionals sharing insights and experiences.

Periodic

Mock Interview Sessions

Practice interviews conducted by HR professionals and faculty.
