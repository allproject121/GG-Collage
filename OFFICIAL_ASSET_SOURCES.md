### How to Replace

1. Obtain photographs from the college administration
2. Save them under `public/images/official/` with descriptive names
3. Update `<img>` `src` attributes in the relevant HTML files
4. Recommended format: WebP or optimized JPEG, max 200KB per image
5. Always include `alt` text and `loading="lazy"`
